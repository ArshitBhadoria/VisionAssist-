💡 Spoken Voice Commands Guide

Natural speech recognized
"Describe my surroundings" / "What's in front of me?" Runs YOLO11n object detection and speaks spatial scene narration.
"Read this text" / "Read" Extracts printed/handwritten text using EasyOCR and reads it aloud.
"Where am I?" / "What is my location?" Obtains GPS coordinates and speaks your locality, street, and city.
"Start the camera" / "Stop the camera" Turns the video camera on or off to preserve power and privacy.
"Repeat that" / "Say again" Repeats the previous spoken narration verbatim.
"Stop speaking" / "Quiet" Immediately pauses and halts any currently active voice output.
